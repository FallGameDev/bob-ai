Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Bob AI is a study helper that runs in your web browser. This page explains, in plain words, what information Bob uses and what happens to it.

When you sign in

You can sign in with Google, GitHub, or your email. Sign in is handled by Supabase. Bob only gets your name, email address, and profile picture. Bob never sees your Google or GitHub password.

Your chats stay on your device

Your chats, projects, settings, and any files you upload are saved in your own browser on your device. They are not sent to FallGameDev or stored on a server. Signing out or clearing your browser data removes them from that device.

When Bob looks things up

To answer some questions, your browser asks public websites for information, like Wikipedia, Wikidata, OpenStreetMap, Iconify, Wikimedia Commons, and GitHub. Only the words needed for that search are sent, like the name of a topic. If you add your own YouTube API key in Settings, it stays on your device and is only sent to YouTube.

Your location

If you ask for places "near me", your browser asks you first before sharing your location. It is only used to find nearby places and is not saved.

Bug reports

If you use Report a bug, it opens your own email app with the report filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send. The report includes basic browser info (like your browser and screen size) to help fix the bug.

What Bob does not do

Bob does not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other websites.

Kids

Bob is made for students. If you are under 13, please use Bob with a parent or guardian. A parent can ask us to delete an account by emailing the address below.

Deleting your account

Email aidankadrovic2@gmail.com to delete your account. To remove your chats from a device, open Settings in Bob and press Clear all chats.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
